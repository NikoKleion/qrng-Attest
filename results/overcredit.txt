  0.650    0.641   yes           0.6293     0.0170       +0.6002
  0.750    0.740   yes           0.4247     0.0145       +0.3956

  0.950    0.932   yes           0.0974     0.0247       +0.0682
  0.999    0.979   yes           0.0282     0.0268       -0.0010

  the observed stream is exactly Bernoulli-IID at every point: it passes the IID screen and no
  output-only statistical test can distinguish it from a genuine source of the same bias.
  the black-box track credits up to 0.858 bits/bit against a true source
  entropy of 0.0291 (over-credit 0.829 bits/bit at F = 0.550);
  the attested bound stays at or below the truth at every point.
  detector noise is characterized classical noise and is not credited as quantum source entropy.

Crosstalk over-credit experiment: per-stream SP 800-90B vs the joint rate
  qubit B copies qubit A with probability c; both marginals are uniform-IID; 60000 shots per point

  c       truth rate   per-stream credit   1-MI debit   joint bound
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  0.05        0.9648              0.9826       0.9980        0.9486  <-- 1-MI over-credits
  0.20        0.8685              0.8403       0.9703        0.8456  <-- 1-MI over-credits
  0.35        0.7835              0.8572       0.9091        0.7710  <-- 1-MI over-credits
  0.50        0.7075              0.9824       0.8157        0.6988  <-- 1-MI over-credits
  0.80        0.5760              0.9798       0.4692        0.5675

  every marginal stream passes per-stream assessment at close to 1 bit/bit; the true joint
  rate is lower at every c > 0. The 1-MI debit exceeds the truth under moderate crosstalk
  (it is a Shannon quantity); the worst-pair joint bound stays at or below the truth
  everywhere, so it is the debit the attestation uses.

Mis-calibration sensitivity: the attested bound vs calibration error
  actual readout fidelity 0.75, true source entropy 0.0291 bits/bit; the attestation assumes fidelity F_actual + dF
  'boxed' propagates the calibration's own 2000-shot statistical margin (about 0.026) into the bound

  dF        assumed F   point attested   boxed attested   point over-credit   boxed over-credit
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  -0.05        0.700           0.0000           0.0000             -0.0291           -0.0291
  -0.02        0.730           0.0000           0.0000             -0.0291           -0.0291
  +0.00        0.750           0.0209           0.0000             -0.0083           -0.0291
  +0.02        0.770           0.0745           0.0079             +0.0454 <-- over-credits           -0.0213
  +0.05        0.800           0.1445           0.0845             +0.1153 <-- over-credits           +0.0554 <-- over-credits

  with the calibration taken as exact, the attested value exceeds the true entropy when the
  assumed fidelity is above the real one. With the calibration's statistical margin included,
  the bound holds for any error inside that margin. An overstatement larger than the margin
  exceeds the true entropy in both columns.
